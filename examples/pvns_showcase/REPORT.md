## osteoclast-pvns-specificity

**Claim:** Osteoclast cluster is essentially PVNS-only: 407 cells in pvns-c, 101 in pvns-l, 0 in huamo (control).

**Biological interpretation under test:** Osteoclast-like multinucleated giant cells are a hallmark of PVNS / tenosynovial giant cell tumour (TGCT) and are largely absent from normal/control synovium.

**Data check** - [REPRODUCED]: recomputed osteoclast/group = {'huamo': 0, 'pvns-c': 407, 'pvns-l': 101}; claimed {'pvns-c': 407, 'pvns-l': 101, 'huamo': 0}

**Literature** - [SUPPORTED] (confidence: high)

- Multinucleated cells in PVNS and GCTTS express features of osteoclasts (Darling) (1997). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1858182/
- Giant cells in PVNS express osteoclast phenotype incl. lacunar resorption (Neale) (1997). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9306944/
- Osteoclast-like giant cells in TGCT from CD14+ monocytes (Robert/Miossec) (2022). https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2022.820046/full
- PVNS - StatPearls (osteoclast-type giant cells characteristic) (2024). https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK549850/

_Caveat:_ Giant cells are abundance-variable: less common in the diffuse form and rare/absent in ~20% of TGCT cases, so absence does not exclude TGCT - but presence still discriminates PVNS from normal synovium, so the claim's direction holds.

## global-directional-rescue

**Claim:** Across cell types, disease-altered genes reverse direction under treatment far above chance (macrophages 67.3% opposite, sign-test p=3e-9; fibroblasts 80.7%, p~1e-47), evidencing a global transcriptional rescue despite limited per-gene power.

**Biological interpretation under test:** An effective therapy is expected to reverse the disease transcriptional program direction across genes; a genome-wide directional reversal (sign test) is a valid readout when individual genes are underpowered.


## treatment-own-signature

**Claim:** Treated vs control still differs in many genes (e.g. 730 DEGs in macrophages), so photodynamic treatment does not fully restore the control transcriptome - it imposes its own signature on top of partial disease reversal.

**Biological interpretation under test:** Photodynamic therapy generates reactive oxygen species and induces oxidative-stress, cell-death and inflammatory transcriptional programs, producing a treatment-specific signature distinct from untreated tissue.


## ambient-myeloid-in-nonmyeloid

**Claim:** Macrophage markers (CD163, MARCO, AIF1, C1QB, MMP9) appear as disease-up genes in the fibroblast, EC and T pseudobulks, consistent with ambient-RNA / contamination from the massive macrophage expansion rather than genuine expression by those cell types.

**Biological interpretation under test:** Ambient (cell-free) RNA contamination in droplet scRNA-seq causes highly expressed genes from an abundant cell type to appear in other cell types' profiles, especially when that cell type dominates the sample.


## mac-expansion-not-artifact

**Claim:** The macrophage expansion is not a dimensionality-reduction artifact: an embedding-free macrophage-marker-positive fraction (control 4.8% / disease 40.5% / treated 10.0%) correlates r=0.998 with the cluster-based fraction, and a full best-practice Harmony-integrated re-clustering reproduces it (7.9% / 44.0% / 14.7%).

**Biological interpretation under test:** A macrophage-rich infiltrate is the established histological hallmark of PVNS/TGCT, so a large macrophage fraction in the lesion is biologically expected and should be robust to pipeline choices.

## macrophage-doublet-fraction

**Claim:** About 28% of cells coarsely annotated as macrophages fall into doublet/contamination subclusters expressing fibroblast (COL1A2/COL6A3), endothelial (VWF/EMCN) or B-cell (CD79A/MS4A1) programs, and should be removed before per-cell macrophage DGE.

**Biological interpretation under test:** Residual doublets and ambient-RNA contamination are expected in droplet scRNA-seq, especially in samples dominated by one abundant cell type, and subclustering is a standard way to identify and exclude them.


## collagen-high-mac-doublets

**Claim:** Macrophage subclusters co-expressing a full fibroblast program (COL1A2/COL6A3/CALD1/SOX5) are doublet-enriched: Scrublet (per sample) confirms 34.6% doublets in the collagen-high subcluster and 47% in the endothelial-gene subcluster, vs ~0% in genuine macrophage states and 4.2% baseline. Removing doublets does not change the macrophage expansion (pvns-c 41.4->41.2%).

**Biological interpretation under test:** A macrophage cluster co-expressing a full, coherent fibroblast program (structural collagens plus lowly-expressed non-secreted genes such as SOX5/CALD1) is best interpreted as fibroblast-macrophage doublets, NOT a genuine collagen-producing macrophage. A real pro-fibrotic / scar-associated macrophage population exists but is defined by TREM2/SPP1/CD9 and PDGFB (promoting fibroblast collagen), not by transcribing collagen itself.

**Literature** - [SUPPORTED] (confidence: high)

- Resolving the fibrotic niche of human liver cirrhosis - TREM2+/SPP1+ scar-associated macrophages (Ramachandran) (2019). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31597160/
- Macrophage collagen endocytosis via uPARAP/Endo180 & mannose receptor (Madsen/Melander) (2015). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26316068/
- COL1A1 in BAL macrophages tracks progressive ILD (Tsitoura) (2021). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34867934/
- scRNA-seq of macrophages - facts and artifacts (foreign-transcript contamination) (2023). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37595274/
- DecontX ambient RNA decontamination (Yang) (2020). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7059395/

_Caveat:_ Data + literature converge: full fibroblast-program co-expression (incl. non-secreted SOX5/CALD1, which ambient RNA does not transfer) = doublets; Scrublet confirms 34.6-47% in those clusters vs ~0% in real macrophage states. Genuine pro-fibrotic macrophages (TREM2+/SPP1+/CD9+ scar-associated, Ramachandran 2019) act via PDGFB, not collagen transcription, and in this dataset are the separate lipid/foamy TAM cluster. Macrophage collagen *protein* endocytosis (uPARAP/MRC1) and partial/selective expression (type VI; isolated COL1A1 in ILD) are real but do not explain a full structural-collagen mRNA program.
